How Much Does a Business Website Cost in Kenya?

Updated 15 November 2026 · Practical guidance for Kenyan businesses

One of the first questions business owners ask before hiring a web designer is: how much does a website cost in Kenya? The honest answer is that the price depends on what the website must do, how much content it needs and whether it requires custom features. A simple business website and a full online shop are different projects, so they should not be priced as if they were the same.

What determines website design cost?

1. Number of pages. A focused website with Home, About, Services and Contact pages generally takes less work than a large site with many service pages, locations, resources and landing pages. Each page needs a clear purpose, useful copy and a good mobile layout.

2. Design and functionality. A custom layout, booking form, customer dashboard, product catalogue, search, quotation calculator or integration requires more planning and development than a basic information page.

3. E-commerce and payments. Online shops need product management, cart and checkout flows, order notifications, delivery or collection options, inventory handling and payment processing. M-Pesa STK Push integration also needs secure server-side handling and proper payment confirmation.

4. Content and SEO. Copywriting, image preparation, keyword research, metadata, structured data and redirects all affect the scope. A website should be planned to help customers and search engines understand the business from launch.

What should a professional website include?

  • Responsive design that works on phones, tablets and desktops.
  • Clear service or product information and visible contact options.
  • Fast-loading, compressed images and accessible navigation.
  • Unique page titles, meta descriptions and sensible heading structure.
  • HTTPS, secure forms, backups and reliable hosting configuration.
  • Analytics and Google Search Console setup where appropriate.

Should I choose the cheapest website quote?

Compare deliverables rather than the headline price alone. Ask who owns the domain, whether hosting and maintenance are included, how many revisions are allowed, whether the site is editable, and whether you receive the source code or handover details. Request a written scope so both sides understand what is included and what would cost extra.

How do I choose the right website package?

Start with the business outcome. If customers mainly need to understand your services and contact you, a professional company website may be enough. If you sell products, you may need e-commerce. If customers book appointments, request quotes or track orders, plan those workflows before development begins.
