What Should I Know Before Adding M-Pesa to an E-commerce Website?

Updated 15 November 2026 · Practical guidance for Kenyan businesses

For a Kenyan online shop, a familiar payment option can make checkout more convenient. Business owners often ask whether they should add M-Pesa to their website and what is involved. The important point is that a payment prompt alone is not a complete checkout system: the website must also confirm the transaction securely and connect it to the correct order.

What is M-Pesa STK Push?

STK Push allows a customer to receive a payment prompt on their phone after entering the number they want to pay with. The customer reviews and authorises the transaction on the handset. The website or backend then receives payment status information through the configured callback process.

Why must payment confirmation happen on the server?

A browser redirect or a message displayed on the customer's screen should not be treated as proof of payment. The backend should validate callback data, match the transaction to the pending order, verify the amount and reference, and update the order state only after a confirmed successful payment. Credentials and sensitive Daraja configuration belong on the server, not in public frontend code.

What should a reliable checkout flow include?

  1. Show the order summary, delivery or collection choice and total amount before payment.
  2. Collect and validate the customer's phone number and order details.
  3. Create a pending order with a unique reference before initiating payment.
  4. Send the STK Push request securely from the backend.
  5. Process callbacks safely and prevent duplicate updates.
  6. Show a clear pending, successful or failed state to the customer.
  7. Provide an order reference and a way to contact the business if help is needed.

What about failed or delayed payments?

Networks can be slow, customers can cancel prompts and callbacks can arrive later than expected. The checkout should explain that payment is pending rather than claiming success too early. Provide a safe way to check status and avoid creating duplicate charges when a customer retries. Keep logs for troubleshooting without exposing personal payment information unnecessarily.

Do I need inventory and delivery management?

If you sell physical products, the wider store should handle stock, product variations, delivery fees, pickup options, order notifications and fulfilment status. Payment integration is only one part of the customer journey. Test the entire process from product selection to order completion on real mobile devices before launch.

How do I choose an e-commerce developer?

Ask how they protect API credentials, verify callbacks, handle duplicate notifications, manage failed payments and support order reconciliation. Request a demonstration of the complete customer flow and clarify maintenance responsibilities.
